CORNICHE RUN OUTFITS AND FACES: INSTALL
Project: "Wellbe W40 Backend" (Apps Script). About 10 minutes.
Three server files change: RunGame, Levels and Config.

WHAT PLAYERS GET
 * YOUR RUNNER has two new sections: OUTFIT and FACE.
 * 11 outfits: Wellbe Coach Kit, Ramadan Night Run, Yalla Lebnan, Mar Mikhael Street, Modest Mover,
   Rehab Hero, 5:30 AM Club, Race Day 42K, and the 3 prestige outfits Tyrian Purple, Golden Cedar
   and Corniche Legend. "Custom" keeps the old Top, Bottom and Shoes pickers.
 * Every outfit can be tried on. A locked outfit shows how to unlock it and cannot be used to run.
 * Face: 8 skin tones, 4 eye colours. While sprinting the runner makes a Game Face,
   after 10 coins in a row a Happy face.

HOW OUTFITS UNLOCK (checked on the server, the phone only shows it)
 * Yalla Lebnan, Modest Mover: free.
 * Wellbe Coach Kit: 1,500 coins. Free for staff (the player's email or phone matches a row in STAFF,
   or their client ID is in the setting game.staffClients).
 * Ramadan Night Run: 1,000 coins (can be bought any time).
 * Mar Mikhael Street: 2,000 coins.
 * Rehab Hero: free if the client has a Physio, Prehab or Assessment package in PACKAGES, otherwise 1,000 coins.
 * 5:30 AM Club: 10 runs of at least 300 m that started before 7:00 (Beirut time). Counting starts after this update.
 * Race Day 42K: 42 km run in total (all runs, also the ones before this update).
 * Tyrian Purple: reach level 20, then 3,000 coins. Golden Cedar: level 30, then 6,000.
   Corniche Legend: level 40, then 10,000. (These are the old "Outfit 1/2/3" shop slots.
   They are no longer free at that level: the level opens them, the coins buy them.)
 Prices and rules are in Config (game.outfits) and can be changed in SETTINGS like the other game numbers.

A. SERVER FIRST
 1. Open the "Wellbe W40 Backend" project in Apps Script.
 2. Open the file RunGame. Click inside it, press Ctrl+A (select all), then Delete.
    Open RunGame.gs from this folder, select all, copy, and paste it into RunGame. Press Ctrl+S.
 3. Do the same for Levels (paste Levels.gs) and Config (paste Config.gs). Press Ctrl+S each time.
 4. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil (Edit) > Version: New version > Deploy.
    Same link, nothing to send to anyone.
    The app on the phones keeps working the same with this server (it simply ignores the outfits).

B. THEN THE APPS (GitHub Desktop, after step 4)
 5. Changed files: client/index.html, client/run.html and client/run-test.html. Tick them, Commit, Push.
    (index.html lets the game buy outfits. Phones that still have the app open must close and reopen it once.)
    If the app comes out before the server: outfits show as locked, faces work.

C. QUICK CHECKS (on your phone, after step 5)
 6. Open the game, tap your runner to change it. OUTFIT shows Custom first, then 11 cards.
    Yalla Lebnan and Modest Mover say Free. Pick one, START RUNNING.
 7. Pick Ramadan Night Run: the runner wears it, the box says "Try it on here. Unlock: 1,000 coins".
    With enough coins tap "Buy for 1,000 coins", then tap again to confirm. The card now says Yours.
 8. Sprint in a run: the face turns into the Game Face. Pick up a long row of coins: Happy face.
 9. Your own row in CORNICHE RUN, column U (Owned), now has "of_ramadan":1 after that buy.

IF A STAFF MEMBER DOES NOT GET THE COACH KIT FREE
 Their email or phone in the client list must match the STAFF tab, or add their client ID to SETTINGS:
   game.staffClients    ["C012"]
 The check is kept for 3 hours, so it can take up to 3 hours to show.
