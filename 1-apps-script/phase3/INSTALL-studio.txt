WHAT CHANGES FOR PLAYERS

   Female / Male sits on the runner preview. The camera zooms to what you are changing.

   and on the leaderboards. Rude names (English and Lebanese, in Latin letters or Arabic) are refused.
   No name picked = the first name, like before ("Majd B." on the boards until they pick one).
 - Face: 8 faces per body (Classic, Sharp, Soft, Bold Brows, Freckles, Bright Eyes, Strong, and
   Glow for women / Chiselled for men), with the 8 skin tones and 4 eye colours. All free.
 - Hair: the same styles, colours and beard as before.
 - Gear: hats (Cap, Headband free; Backwards Cap, Visor, Beanie, Bucket Hat), eyewear (Sport Shades free;
   Round Shades, Aviators), ears (Earbuds free; Headphones), wrist (Sweatband free; Sport Watch).
   A hat tucks the hair in.
 - Outfit: the 10 outfits and Custom, unchanged.
 - Tattoos: 9 designs (Heartbeat and Stars free; Wellbe Logo, Roman Numerals XLII, Cedar, Tribal Band, Rose,
   Quwwa, Yalla) on 5 spots (forearm, upper arm, shoulder, calf, back of neck). They only show on bare skin.
 - Taunt: the move the runner does at the start line during 3 2 1. Yes! is free. 13 more cost coins
   (Dance, Hip Hop, Butterfly Twirl, Capoeira and Roll also need a level first).
 - Sprint intervals are now 45 seconds (were 60).

A. SERVER FIRST
 1. Open the "Wellbe W40 Backend" project in Apps Script.
 2. Open the file RunGame. Click inside it, press Ctrl+A (select all), then Delete.
 3. Open RunGame.gs from this folder, select all, copy, and paste it into RunGame. Press Ctrl+S.
 4. Do the same for Config: open Config, Ctrl+A, Delete, paste Config.gs from this folder, Ctrl+S.
    (If you changed any setting in Config by hand since the last update, put it back after pasting.)
 5. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil (Edit) > Version: New version > Deploy.
    Same link, nothing to send to anyone.
    Old phones keep working: they ignore names and studio items, the leaderboards already show the new names.

B. THEN THE APPS (GitHub Desktop, after step 5)
 6. Changed files: client/run.html, client/run-test.html, and the new folder client/models/taunts/ (13 files).
    Also new: tools/taunts/ (the converter, not used by the app). Tick everything, Commit, Push.
    If the apps go first by mistake: nothing breaks. The free items work and the paid ones say "Opens soon".

C. QUICK CHECKS (on your phone, after step 6)
 7. Open the game. Under the runner you see the tabs. Tap Face: the camera zooms on the face.
 8. Name tab: type a name, tap Save. It shows above the runner. Play one run: your name is on the leaderboard.
    Try a rude word: it is refused with "Pick another name, that one is not allowed."
 9. Gear: tap Cap (free), then Backwards Cap: it shows "Buy for 600 coins". Tap it twice to buy.
    The coins go down and the Owned column (U) of your CORNICHE RUN row has "g_capb":1.
10. Taunt: pick Yes! (free) and start a run: the runner does it during 3 2 1.
11. Sprints now last 45 seconds (the SPRINT bar at the top counts down from 45).

ADDING MORE TAUNTS LATER
 tools/taunts/README.md explains it: download from Mixamo (Y Bot, FBX, Without Skin), add one line to
