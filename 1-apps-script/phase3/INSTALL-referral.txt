INVITE A FRIEND: $5 REFERRAL CREDIT (Oct 26): INSTALL
Project: "Wellbe W40 Backend" (Apps Script). About 10 minutes.
ONE new server file (Referral) and THREE changed (App, Client, Config). No column of an existing tab is added or moved.
One new tab, REFERRALS, is made by itself in the Operations sheet the first time a client sends an invite.

WHAT CHANGES
 - Client app, "Who would you like to invite to Wellbe?" card: new line
   "If your friend joins Wellbe, you get $5 Wellbe Credit for your next package."
   Under it, "Your invites": each friend with Invited, Joined (they became a lead, guest or client) or +$5 earned,
   and "$5 credit ready" when the client has wallet credit. Inviting your own number is refused.
 - Every invite is saved in REFERRALS (who invited, friend name, friend phone without spaces, +961, 961 or leading 0,
   date, status). Romy still gets the invite in her To do, now with the referral number and whether it earns credit.
 - When an invited friend buys their FIRST PAID package (not the free trial, not a free package, price above $0),
   the client who invited them gets $5 in CLIENT WALLET by itself:
   Amount 5, Reason "Referral: <friend name>", Package = the friend's package, By "auto".
   They also get a message in the app ("Sara joined Wellbe. $5 Wellbe Credit is ready for your next package.").
   Rules: once per friend phone. Two clients invite the same friend: the first one gets it.
   No credit for yourself, and none when the friend already had a paid package before the invite.
 - Staff app (Romy, Majd):
   * Sell a package to an invited friend: "Referred by Lara Khoury: $5 credit will be added to Lara."
     (also in the "Sell and book" panel). After the sale the message says "$5 credit added to Lara".
   * Sell a package to a client with wallet credit: "Wallet credit $5 ... Use it on this package" (ticked).
     It is taken off what is left to pay (the live Wallet.gs does it, same as overpaid money). Untick to keep it.
   * Client card: a "Referrals" box: who referred them, who they invited, their wallet credit.
 - Wallet credit is real money for packages. It is NOT the game's Cash or coins (those stay in CORNICHE RUN).

THE AMOUNT
 Setting referral.amount (default 5). To change it: SETTINGS tab, Key referral.amount, Value e.g. 10.
 0 turns referrals off (nothing is given, the card line is hidden). Run cfgBump() to see it at once.

A. SERVER FIRST (before the apps)
 1. Open the "Wellbe W40 Backend" project in Apps Script.
 2. NEW FILE: Files "+" > Script > name it Referral (the editor adds .gs). Paste Referral.gs from this folder. Ctrl+S.
 3. Open App. Ctrl+A, Delete, paste App.gs from this folder, Ctrl+S.
 4. Open Client. Ctrl+A, Delete, paste Client.gs from this folder, Ctrl+S.
 5. Open Config. Ctrl+A, Delete, paste Config.gs from this folder, Ctrl+S.
    (If you changed a setting in Config by hand since the last update, put it back after pasting.
     Settings in the SETTINGS tab are kept, they are not in the file.)
    Wallet is not changed, leave it.
 6. Run once (dropdown at the top, Run):
      cfgSetup   -> adds the row referral.amount = 5 to the SETTINGS tab (other rows are not touched)
      refSetup   -> makes the REFERRALS tab now and logs the amount (optional, it is made by itself anyway)
 7. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil (Edit) > Version: New version > Deploy.
    Same link, nothing to send to anyone.

B. THEN THE APPS (GitHub Desktop, after step 7)
 8. Changed: index.html (staff app), client/index.html (client app). Tick both, Commit, Push.
    Old app versions keep working with the new server (they just do not show the new parts).
    A new app with the old server shows the card line but nothing is saved: that is why the server goes first.

C. QUICK CHECKS
 9. Client app (your own test account): Center > the invite card shows the $5 line. Invite a friend with a
    test number: Romy gets the request, REFERRALS has a row "Invited". Inviting your own number says
    "This is your own number. Invite a friend instead."
10. Staff app: make that test number a client, sell a free trial: nothing. Sell a paid package: the sell screen
    showed "Referred by ...", CLIENT WALLET has + 5 "Referral: <name>" By auto, REFERRALS says Rewarded.
    Sell another paid package to the same friend: no second credit.
11. Delete your test rows (REFERRALS, CLIENT WALLET, the test packages) when done.

PACKAGES TYPED BY HAND
 A package typed straight into the PACKAGES tab (not sold in the app) is checked at the friend's next app sale.
 To give those credits right away: run refCheckAll from the editor (it only gives credit that is due, never twice).
